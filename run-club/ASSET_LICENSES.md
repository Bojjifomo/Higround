# Asset licences

The repo is public. Third-party art whose licence forbids redistribution is **never committed**: it lives in
`run-club/assets/vendor/` (gitignored), synced from private Supabase Storage. Only **CC0 / Public Domain**
and **Higround-owned** assets are committed under `run-club/assets/own/` and `run-club/apps/web/public/assets/`.
